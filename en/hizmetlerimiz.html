Services

End-to-end support in four blocks

You may need one block or all four. Deliverables and timelines are written up front for each.

1. University counselling

We filter European bachelor’s or master’s programmes to your profile and move with 3–6 workable options. Lists don’t balloon; every school gets a note on why it fits — or why it doesn’t.

  • Fit analysis by GPA, language certificate and budget
  • Subject + country strategy (English-taught vs local-language programmes)
  • Application calendar and deadline tracking
  • SOP / motivation letter structure and feedback

2. Language school placement

General English, academic English or IELTS/TOEFL prep in the UK, Spain, France and elsewhere in Europe. You compare course length, weekly hours and housing type side by side.

  • School suggestions by level and goal
  • Course + residence / host-family enrolment
  • Tracking acceptance letters and payment schedules

3. Application & document management

Missing pages or bad translations are the most common reasons applications stall. We run transcripts, diplomas, references and recognition steps with a checklist.

  • Guidance on transcripts / diplomas / recognition
  • Reference letter review
  • Tracking translation and certification steps

4. Visa and after arrival

The file doesn’t close when acceptance arrives. Visa documents, appointments, insurance and a first-week checklist stay in the same email thread.

  • Visa file preparation and appointment tracking
  • Health insurance and housing guidance
  • Pre-departure orientation and a short post-arrival briefing

Which block do you need?

Just write “university / language school / visa only” in your message. We’ll clarify scope and estimated timing for you.
