About

We don’t sell schools; we build application files

Birikim Danışmanlık grew out of 16 years in the classroom and education management. Goal: clarify the European programme that actually fits you.

Founder Birikim Büyükgebiz spent years teaching English and leading education teams. We don’t start with “which school pays commission?” — we start with “can this student enter this programme, is the language level enough, is the budget realistic?”

Our base is Alanya. Processes can open online from Muratpaşa, Kepez, Konyaaltı and anywhere in Turkey. For those who prefer in person, we book appointments in Alanya.

What we promise

A realistic school list, complete document checks and answers from one person. Your file does not fall into a sales funnel — it stays in an email thread.

What we don’t do

Pushing unsuitable programmes, inflated acceptance guarantees or verbal “all-inclusive” packages. We don’t start work until scope is confirmed in writing.

How the process works

1) Target country, programme type and budget arrive by form or email. 2) Within 1–3 business days we write a preliminary review: strengths, gaps and possible routes. 3) If we agree, you get a school list, document checklist and calendar. 4) After application and acceptance we continue visa / housing steps with the same contact.

We are a formally registered education consultancy. Our aim is not to sell the most expensive school — it is to build a European plan consistent with your inputs.
