FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which countries do you advise on?

Our focus countries are Spain, France and the UK. If another European programme fits your profile, we manage that application with the same file discipline.

How long does the process take?

First reply usually within 1–3 business days. Language school: often 1–3 weeks. University: typically 4–12 weeks depending on documents and intake. We put timelines in writing from the start.

Is an in-person meeting required?

No. You can proceed by email and online calls from anywhere in Turkey. If you live in Alanya, we can also meet face to face by appointment.

How do fees work?

Scope and fees are confirmed in writing once your file is clear. We don’t sell a vague “all-inclusive” package — you see every included step item by item.

Why is contact mainly by email?

Acceptance, visa and document steps need a written record. Our main channel is info@birikimedu.com — every decision stays in one thread.
